NCAA All-Sports Rosters 2025-26

513,655 college athletes · all 28 NCAA sports · 3 divisions · both genders · named + enriched · the House v. NCAA "year zero"

Hugging Face (DOI) Kaggle DOI: 10.57967/hf/9512

Individual-level roster data for all 28 NCAA championship and emerging sports, all three divisions (D1/D2/D3), both genders, athletic year 2025-26 — the first year under the House v. NCAA settlement (roster limits, revenue sharing, NIL). Covers ~91.6% of officially reported NCAA participations. Named and enriched (v2.1.0): first/last name, height, weight, academic major, and previous school as published by each school, plus per-sport season-stats sidecar files for 10 sports (205,132 athletes). Every field is an institution-published roster fact validated against the NCAA's official sponsor lists.
